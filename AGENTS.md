# Course editing guidance

For lecture, notebook, outline and course-documentation work, read and apply
[course-lecture-review](skills/course-lecture-review/SKILL.md).
It records the instructor's teaching and editing preferences established while
revising Weeks 1–4. New instructions from the instructor take precedence.

The revised Weeks 1–4 are the reference for tone, progression and detail. Preserve
their accepted explanations and figures unless the requested change concerns them.
Read a week's review notes, when available, before revising its materials.
